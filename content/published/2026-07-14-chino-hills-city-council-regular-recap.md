The tax would increase the City’s rate from 7.75% to 8.75% and is estimated to generate approximately $11.5 million annually, with all revenue staying local [source](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/393508?ts=2587) [source](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/393508?ts=2568). The council also voted 5-0 to authorize all five members to sign written arguments for the measure and to direct the City Attorney to prepare an impartial analysis [source](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/393508?ts=3730) [source](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/393508?ts=3783).
