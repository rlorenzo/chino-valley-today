The Chino City Council met on July 21, 2026, at 4:30 p.m. for closed session and 6:00 p.m. for open session, presided over by Mayor Eunice M. Ulloa. The Council took action on a business license tax measure for the November ballot, approved fee schedule updates, and awarded contracts for park irrigation design and pavement improvements [Meeting Detail](https://chino.legistar.com/MeetingDetail.aspx?LEGID=1963&GID=931&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315).

### Business License Reform and Small Business Protection Measure​

The Council voted unanimously to place a measure on the November 3, 2026, ballot asking voters to approve a new business license tax structure. The proposed measure would replace the current system (last significantly updated in 1987) with a gross-receipts-based model: businesses with $75,000 or less in annual gross receipts would pay a flat $25 annually; larger businesses would pay 60 cents to $1.50 per $1,000 of gross receipts above $75,000, depending on business category. The measure is estimated to generate approximately $4.8 million in additional annual revenue, bringing total annual business license revenue to approximately $5.9 million. The Council also approved appropriating $100,000 from the current year General Fund budget surplus for election and voter information activities [Business License Reform and Small Business Protection Measure](https://chino.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8140863&GUID=E04E6AC8-75F9-4011-AAE0-5158D9A17997&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315&Options=&Search=).

### Adoption of a Resolution 2026-050 Amending the City's Master User Fee Schedule

The Council adopted Resolution 2026-050, amending the Master Fee Schedule to establish new fees, revise Community Services fees based on updated cost calculations and market analysis, and adjust select fees that require modifications beyond the annual CPI adjustment. As part of the motion, the Council directed staff to delete fees DS02000 and DS02010—the resident appeal fees for Planning Commission and City Council—which the Council determined should not be charged to residents [Adoption of a Resolution 2026-050](https://chino.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8140808&GUID=A906B185-D8F0-455D-A87A-9429F3638B5E&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315&Options=&Search=).

### Award Construction Contract to R.J. Noble Company for Pavement Improvements - Zone 1 & Cypress Avenue Project (ST260)

The Council voted unanimously to award a construction contract to R.J. Noble Company for $852,884 for Project ST260, which includes pavement improvements in Zone 1 and on Cypress Avenue. The project budget is $974,993, which includes a $319,093 transfer from the Chino Hills Parkway Rehabilitation Project (R7231).
