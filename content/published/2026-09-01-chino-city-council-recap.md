### Consider the Applicant’s appeal of the Planning Commission’s June 17, 2026 action on the 210-unit rental housing project located at the northwest corner of Riverside Drive and Mountain Avenue (6210 Riverside Drive), which received a 3-3 vote and, therefore, did not result in an approval or denial of the project.
