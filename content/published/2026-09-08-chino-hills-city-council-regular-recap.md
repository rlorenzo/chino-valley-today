The Chino Hills City Council held a regular meeting on September 8, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. The council approved a major amendment to the Bizpark Project to allow a Chick-fil-A, adopted a new five-year Measure I spending plan, and authorized several contracts and agreements [agenda page 2](https://agendaquick.chinohills.org:8086/docs/2026/CC/20260908_1182/1182_09-08-2026%20CC%20Agenda.pdf#page=2).

### Adopt resolution adopting Addendum No. 2 to previously adopted Mitigated Negative... (Bizpark Chick-fil-A)
The council approved a resolution adopting Addendum No. 2 to the Mitigated Negative Declaration, approving Site Plan Review 07SPR02 Second Amendment (SPR-003-2025), and approving Conditional Use Permit CUP-0004-2025 to allow a Chick-fil-A restaurant with a drive-through at the Bizpark Project, located on the eastern side of Pomona Rincon Road between Chino Hills High School and State Route 71 [agenda item 21](https://agendaquick.chinohills.org:8086/docs/2026/CC/20260908_1182/1182_09-08-2026%20CC%20Agenda.pdf#page=4). The amendment reduces Building 18 from 18,000 square feet to roughly 5,300 square feet and adds a double-lane drive-through with 41 queuing spaces, a 30% buffer over the 27 vehicles observed at three similar Chick-fil-A locations [video](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/400411?ts=3674). The total project square footage will be reduced from 187,000 to approximately 174,000 square feet [video](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/400411?ts=3976). Traffic improvements include a 100-foot left-turn pocket, a 100-foot refuge/merge lane, and two new traffic signals — one at the high school entry drop-off driveway and one at Picasso Road and Pomona Rincon Road [video](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/400411?ts=4435). The motion to approve passed 5-0 [video](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/400411?ts=5179).

### Adopt resolution adopting Measure I Five-Year Capital Project Needs Analysis for Fiscal...
