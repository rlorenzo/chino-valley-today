The Chino City Council met on September 15, 2026, at the Chino Senior Center, beginning with a closed session at 4:30 p.m. and convening the open session at 6:12 p.m. The council voted 4-1 to approve an appeal and development agreement for the Dirac Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project, sent the East Chino Specific Plan Amendment back to staff, and approved several capital improvement projects [meeting page](https://chino.legistar.com/MeetingDetail.aspx?LEGID=1986&GID=931&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315).

### Introduction of Ordinance No. 2026-012 - East Chino Specific Plan Amendment (PL25-0119)
The council held a public hearing on a request to amend the East Chino Specific Plan to allow homes to front onto loop roads, permit on-street parking on Amsterdam and Aguerre avenues, and reduce the minimum front yard setback to 15 feet for homes with swing-in garages in the RD 4.5 land use designation [agenda item](https://chino.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8209296&GUID=A752722B-69CE-492F-9A67-3C71C611A077&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315&Options=&Search=). Residents raised concerns about traffic safety, the narrowness of Amsterdam Avenue, and the violation of the existing specific plan. The applicant, PLC Communities, argued the amendment was needed for a safer project design [transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMYD715t1PQ&t=2516s). A motion by Council Member Comstock, seconded by Council Member Lucio, to send the item back to staff to work with the applicant and return to the council after re-noticing the public hearing, carried unanimously [minutes](https://chino.legistar.com/MeetingDetail.aspx?LEGID=1986&GID=931&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315).

### Appeal of the Planning Commission Action Denying the Dirac Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Project
The council considered an appeal of the Planning Commission’s denial of the Dirac BESS project, a 400 megawatt facility on 13.89 acres between Oaks and Benson avenues, north of Edison Avenue [agenda item](https://chino.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8209303&GUID=3F1EF4B5-DDA8-4917-AC7F-F9F6ED0B1897&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315&Options=&Search=). Staff recommended approval, while residents and Mayor Pro Tem Burton raised concerns about fire safety, proximity to residential areas, and potential health impacts [transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMYD715t1PQ&t=4863s). The applicant presented additional safety measures, including a 1,100-foot setback from the nearest home and a $10.6 million development agreement [transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMYD715t1PQ&t=5657s). A motion by Council Member Flores, seconded by Council Member Lucio, to grant the appeal, approve the project entitlements, and introduce Ordinance No. 2026-013, carried 4-1, with Mayor Pro Tem Burton voting no [minutes](https://chino.legistar.com/MeetingDetail.aspx?LEGID=1986&GID=931&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315).

### Establish a New Capital Improvement Program (CIP) Project - Edison Avenue Ultimate Improvements and Cypress Creek Trail Extension
The council approved establishing a CIP project to design improvements on Edison Avenue, including a trail extension and right-of-way evaluation for a future six-lane corridor [agenda item](https://chino.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8209309&GUID=A613D0DB-A46B-48BA-9ACE-D91DD2F8B095&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315&Options=&Search=). A resident spoke about the deteriorating pavement and ground shaking from trucks. The motion, which included appropriating $170,906.50 from the Road Maintenance and Rehabilitation Fund and $200,000 of Measure I funds, and awarding a design agreement to HR Green Pacific, Inc., carried unanimously [minutes](https://chino.legistar.com/MeetingDetail.aspx?LEGID=1986&GID=931&G=FCED2B78-20F3-40ED-B45D-72741543B315).

### Establish a New Capital Improvement Program (CIP) Project - Pavement Improvements Zone 2 (Chino Ave and Various Streets)
