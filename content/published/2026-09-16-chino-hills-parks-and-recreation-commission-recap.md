### Capital Improvement Program Budget for Fiscal Year 2026-27 Presentation

Engineering Manager Carl Hassel gave a PowerPoint presentation, and commissioners asked questions about several projects. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

- Green bike lanes: Hassel said funding comes from a Highway Safety Improvement Program grant and gas tax revenues, and that the lanes would be located at major intersections. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)
- Traffic signal backplates: Hassel said the yellow backplate project was in last year's budget and is nearing completion. He said it is expected to finish by November. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)
- Peyton Drive: Hassel confirmed that resurfacing would begin soon and extend from Grand Avenue to Costco. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)
- Eucalyptus Avenue: Hassel said a project to replace the drainage ditch is in the design phase, and the design must account for existing trees. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)
- Sidewalks: Hassel said the sidewalk replacement program covers replacing existing sidewalks in designated areas, not adding sidewalks in Los Serranos. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

### Recreation Survey Presentation

Community Services Manager Armit presented the survey results. She said the open comments covered a wide variety of topics with no single recurring suggestion, and that staff are reviewing them for suggestions. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

On the 2.5% credit card service fee, Armit said it covers the cost of accepting card payments, is listed separately, and is not charged to residents who pay with cash. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

Commissioner Jackson noted the low number of responses compared with the City's population and recommended seeking input from the Teen Advisory Board and other youth groups. Commissioner Gavela recommended exploring more ways to increase participation and offering the survey in other languages. Armit said Teen Advisory Board members promoted the survey and staffed booths. She also said residents using the QR code could use Google Translate, and that the City's website offers translation options. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=175253&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)
