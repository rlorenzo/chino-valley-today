The Chino Hills Planning Commission met on October 20, 2026, and voted 5-0 on three resolutions recommending that the City Council approve municipal code amendments. The commission convened at 6:00 P.M. and adjourned at 7:15 p.m. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=3943&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

### MUNICIPAL CODE AMENDMENT NO. MCA-0003-2026 – ESTABLISHING TITLE 17, DIVISION OF LAND PROCEDURES

The commission approved Resolution No. PC 2026-11, recommending City Council approval of an ordinance repealing Appendix D of the Municipal Code and adding Title 17. Staff discussion noted that the reorganization was triggered by a need and by a requirement of the previously approved Housing Element. No one spoke at the public hearing. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=3943&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

The commission asked whether site plan expirations could be updated to 48 months with this motion. The Community Development Director recommended keeping the current language so staff and counsel could review newly passed state laws. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=3943&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

### MUNICIPAL CODE AMENDMENT MCA-0001-2026 - SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT CODE CHANGES

The commission approved Resolution No. PC 2026-12, recommending City Council approval of an ordinance amending sections 16.06.120, 16.10.040, 16.10.050, 16.10.150, and 16.58.010, among other changes. The commission's comments included that the updates will improve and streamline single-family development. No one spoke at the public hearing. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=3943&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

### MUNICIPAL CODE AMENDMENT MCA-0002-2026 - CHAPTER 16.76 SITE PLAN REVIEW CHANGES

The commission approved Resolution No. PC 2026-13, recommending City Council approval of an ordinance amending Chapter 16.76. It ties the expiration dates of Conditional Use Permits, Variances, and Zoning Clearances approved concurrently with a site plan to the expiration date of the Site Plan Review. No one spoke at the public hearing. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=3943&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

### PUBLIC COMMENTS

Several residents addressed the commission about the Caltrans road widening project at the S curves on Carbon Canyon Road, and about fire safety and evacuation routes in Carbon Canyon. The Community Development Director said the city has made its comments to Caltrans, which has not yet completed its notice of determination, and that the city has started the process to make the truck restrictions permanent. The commission requested a future agenda discussion item on fire safety. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=3943&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)

The November 17 agenda forecast lists front yard paving, ADU pre-approved plans, and appointment of a new Chair and Vice Chair. [minutes](https://publicportal.chinohills.org/WebLink/Browse.aspx?id=3943&dbid=0&repo=CoCH)
