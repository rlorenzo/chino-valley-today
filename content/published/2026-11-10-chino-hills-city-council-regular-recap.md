The Chino Hills City Council met on November 10, 2026. It proclaimed Chino Hills a Purple Heart City and voted 5-0 to approve a Chino Hills Parkway paving resolution and to introduce a water-conservation ordinance and two sets of land-use ordinances. ### PROCLAMATION - Proclaiming Chino Hills as a Purple Heart City

The mayor said the council was proclaiming Chino Hills a Purple Heart City to honor service members wounded or killed in service. ### Introduce Ordinance to add prohibition of potable water irrigation on nonfunctional turf

A staff member said that under Assembly Bill 1572, the ban on using potable water for nonfunctional turf starts January 1, 2027 for public properties, 2028 for commercial properties and 2029 for homeowner associations. Residential lots are not affected, and city parks and sports fields are exempt. [video](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/408336?ts=2405) [video](https://chinohillsca.new.swagit.com/videos/408336?ts=2611) The introduction passed 5-0. ### Introduce Ordinances for comprehensive update to Single-Family Residential Design and Community Standards

Staff said the amendments add objective design standards for single-family development, create a residential design review chapter, and tie together expiration dates for related entitlements. Staff said the standards are not retroactive. ### Introduce Ordinance for comprehensive update to Subdivision Code
