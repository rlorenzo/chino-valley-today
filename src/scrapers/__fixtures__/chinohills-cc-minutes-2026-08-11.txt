Marquez: Vice Mayor Marquez spoke about the start of the seventh cycle of the
Regional Housing Needs Assessment (RHNA) with the first meeting on August 13,
2026, and pointed out that they used have one representative from each County but
now there are four from Los Angeles County, three each from Orange and Riverside

City Staff to move forward with a truck restriction.

Humane Society (IVHS) will come out if a coyote is injured or not moving and pointed

Manager Hill added that most wildlife experts view animal attacks as normal for coyotes;
therefore, when a domestic pet is attacked, that is considered normal behavior.
